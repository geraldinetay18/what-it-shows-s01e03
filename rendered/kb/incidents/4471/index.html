Why these are two causes

The shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant therefore gets a swapped date through app, email, or a booking-v2 form. By contrast, booking-v3 displays MM/DD/YYYY; for the three day-first tenants, that hint conflicts with the tenant’s date order. The KB’s cause logic assigns those form reports to hint, not parser.

Intake tags do not reliably identify the cause: 23 parser reports were tagged hint, while 15 form-hint reports were tagged parser. The five reports in the desk’s one-day-move note (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620) remain in the parser group; they are not a third cause.
